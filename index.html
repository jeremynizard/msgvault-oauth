msgvault (personal instance)

This Google OAuth client is used by Jeremy Nizard, and only by him, to archive his own Gmail account on his own computer with msgvault, an open-source offline email archiving tool.

It requests read-only access to Gmail. Messages are copied to a local archive on the owner's computer so they can be searched offline.

This is not a public service: no one else is invited or able to use it.

See the privacy policy and the terms of service.
